What it does for you

  • Sets up your brand once Reads your website and any documents you upload, and learns what your company does, who you sell to and how you sound. Everything it writes afterwards uses that.
  • Content Blog posts, landing pages and web copy in your voice, with a final pass so they read like a person wrote them. Publishes straight to WordPress.
  • SEO Keyword research scored by buying intent and clustered, then turned into pages. Connect Search Console and the estimates are replaced by your real impressions, clicks and positions — plus the queries worth acting on.
  • AEO Tracks whether you get cited when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI answers about your category — which prompts you show up for, which you've lost, and who gets cited instead of you.
  • Email outreach that actually sends Multi-step sequences that go out on a schedule from your own domain, with sending limits, warm-up and domain checks handled. Replies, out-of-office notes and bounces are sorted for you, and anyone who replies or unsubscribes stops receiving the rest automatically.
  • Finding and researching people Search by job title, seniority, company size and location, build lists, and get a researched brief on an account before you approach it.
  • Paid ads Ad copy written eight ways against competitors' live ads, audience targeting checked against what the platforms will actually accept, pushed live to Google and Meta from here — then spend, cost per result and return on spend come back in.
  • Social Plan a month at a time with no repeated angles, publish to LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook on schedule, and see what each post did.
  • Campaigns One plan that turns a goal into ordered steps across every channel, each step with its content already written — and campaign shapes that have worked for companies like yours.
  • The whole picture, honestly cut Every channel's contribution to the same goal, with the share nobody can attribute shown rather than quietly folded in.
  • It gets better the longer you use it What performed feeds into the next brief. New work builds on what you've already published instead of repeating it. And you can see what Kepler has learned about your voice and your market as it accumulates.
  • Your team Several people in one workspace, with roles, and a record of who changed what.

Most tools help you make more marketing. Kepler is built to tell you whether it worked.
